# First run and settings

## Confirm the clock

![First-run clock](images/features/clock-setup.png)

Use Left/Right to select a date/time/offset field and Up/Down to change it. Confirm with A. Miami/Eastern is the initial choice: UTC−04:00 in daylight time and UTC−05:00 in standard time. Check the offset as well as the displayed hour. Reopen this picker from **System → Date & Time → Set Clock**.

![Date and time settings](images/features/date-time.png)

Automatic Network Time is optional. Turn it on only when you want online time synchronization and have an internet connection through Wi-Fi or a computer sharing internet over USB.

## Open Settings

![Scrollable settings](images/features/settings.png)

Press F from the shelf to open Settings. Up/Down selects; A opens; B returns; Left/Right adjusts values. Hold a direction to scroll. [Menu map](Menus.md).

The visible menu depends on your installation. Missing SSH, Gallery or a shader page usually means its module or console was not installed. Runtime Features can also disable advanced actions; they do not install missing packages.

## Appearance

![Appearance controls](images/features/appearance.png)

Choose your wallpaper, theme, wallpaper dimming and whether to show Recents. The bundled Quiet Current wallpaper is available without copying a wallpaper file. Your chosen wallpaper also backs Settings and the clock picker. Optional user artwork belongs in the content folders; it is separate from the installed OS.

## Picture and sound

![Screen settings](images/features/screen.png)

Set brightness and the blue-light filter, then configure GB, GBC and GBA independently. [Picture and overlays](Screen-and-Overlays.md) explains scaling and the difference between shaders and Perfect effects.

![Audio settings](images/features/audio.png)

Speaker and wired-headphone volumes are independent. Inserting a 3.5 mm plug routes audio to headphones. Enhanced Audio appears only if installed and starts Off; changes apply on the next game launch. [Gameplay and battery](Gameplay-and-Battery.md).

## Favorites and remembered browsing

![Favorites shelf](images/features/favorites.png)

Press **Y** on the shelf to star a game using the default controls. Favorites collects starred games across the installed consoles; removing a star does not delete the game. **L1/R1** switches shelves by default. Shelf navigation and the favorite action can be remapped in Controls → Shelf Buttons. The launcher remembers the browsed shelf and cartridge across normal shutdowns.

## Controls and restrictions

![Control pages](images/features/controls.png)

Controls groups Console Buttons, Shortcuts and Shelf Buttons. Choose a binding, press A and follow its recording/reset prompts. Resolve conflicts before leaving. [Default controls and combinations](Controls.md).

![Feature restrictions](images/features/features.png)

**System → Features** switches advanced actions On/Off. Save-state browsing and manual states are separate; Favorites, core selection, fast-forward and rewind are independent. Under **Allow In-Game Change**, disable shader, palette or color-correction shortcuts while retaining the chosen picture settings. There is no PIN or game-only preset.

![In-game picture-change restrictions](images/features/features-in-game.png)

## Offline startup and Developer Mode

An ordinary boot starts with optional connections and services off even if you used them previously. Install the desired connection module, then turn it on in Connections when needed. Installing Wi-Fi or SSH does not enable it automatically.

Developer Mode is the explicit exception for development across restarts. It requires SSH and Wi-Fi or USB Networking. Leave it Off for the ordinary offline experience. Public images contain no preconfigured personal Wi-Fi profile or development SSH identity; configure your own access. [Connections](Connections.md).

## Check the result

Open a game, check picture and controls, and use a comfortable volume. Tap F for the pause menu; hold F to eject back to the shelf. Tap Power to sleep and tap again to wake. If you wake from Settings, returning to the shelf is expected.
