# Screenshots and Gallery

These are independent installer modules. Install Screenshots to capture images even if you intend to view them only on a computer. Install Gallery to browse existing images on the handheld; it does not require capture to be installed.

## Capture

![Capture configuration](images/features/screenshots.png)

Press **Select + Start** during a game. Screenshots are PNG files under the SLOT `Screenshots` directory. System → Screenshots independently selects whether captured images include shaders, screen filters and overlays. These choices affect the saved picture, not the live game settings. Confirm a new image in Gallery or by copying it to a computer.

The shortcut can be remapped in Controls. If nothing happens, check that Screenshots was installed and the combination has not been changed or conflicted with another shortcut.

## Gallery

![Screenshot grid sorted by date](images/features/gallery-grid-date.png)

Gallery is a top-level Settings item when installed. Its grid can be sorted **by game, date or platform**; cycle sorting with L/R. A opens the selected image full screen. Left/Right and L/R move between images in full-screen view. B returns.

X requests deletion. The confirmation uses **A to delete** and **B to cancel**. Deletion removes the screenshot, not the game's save or ROM.

Button legends appear when you press controls, fade after two seconds without input, and reappear on the next interaction. This keeps the image unobstructed while viewing. To back up screenshots, use the same card-reader, USB Storage, SMB or SSH routes as other content.

### Step through a capture

1. During a game, press Select + Start. The capture is written in the background.
2. Hold F to eject, open Settings → Gallery and find the new image. Use L/R to cycle Date, Platform and Game sorting.
3. Press A to open it. Use Left/Right or L/R to move to adjacent images; B returns to the grid.
4. Stop pressing buttons for two seconds to let the legends fade. The next input brings them back.
5. To remove an image, press X, read the confirmation, then A to delete or B to keep it.
